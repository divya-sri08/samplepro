7 wonders in the world

SC.NO Image About
1 Great Wall of China is one of the longest man-made structures in the world.

It was built to protect China from invasions and stretches for more than 21,000 km.
2 Petra is an ancient city located in Jordan and is famous for its beautiful rock-cut buildings.

It is also known as the "Rose City" because of its pink-colored stone.
3 Christ the Redeemer is a giant statue of Jesus Christ located in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

It stands 30 meters (98 feet) tall and is a symbol of peace and Christianity.
4 Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city situated in the Andes Mountains of Peru.

It is known for its excellent stone architecture and beautiful mountain scenery.
5 Chichén Itzá is a famous Mayan archaeological site in Mexico.

It is well known for the El Castillo Pyramid, which was used for religious and astronomical purposes.
6 Colosseum is a large ancient amphitheater located in Rome, Italy.

It was used for gladiator fights, public events, and could accommodate around 50,000 spectators.
7 Taj Mahal is a beautiful white marble monument in Agra, India.

It was built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal and is a symbol of love.
